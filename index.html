ABOUT ME

Bridging health science with public health practice.

I'm Olawuwo Idris Adeniyi, a Public Health–oriented Anatomy graduate with a strong interest in health research, epidemiology, community health and health communication.

My academic background in Anatomy has given me a strong foundation in human health and disease, while my practical experiences have expanded my interests toward population health and evidence-based public health practice.

I currently contribute to public health research projects involving literature review, scientific evidence annotation, research writing and manuscript development.

Beyond research, I'm passionate about health communication and youth-focused health initiatives. Through NextGen Health Initiative, I work with others to promote health awareness and community-focused action.
